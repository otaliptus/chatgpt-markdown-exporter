# Privacy Policy

ChatGPT Exporter runs locally in your browser.

- It reads only the active ChatGPT tab when you click the Download or Copy button.
- It does not send conversation content to any server.
- It does not collect analytics.
- It does not store conversation content after the popup closes. Downloaded files and copied text remain on your device until you remove or replace them.
- It uses the browser downloads permission only to save the generated Markdown or LaTeX file.
- It writes to the clipboard only when you choose Copy and never reads existing clipboard content.
